Local modification
------------------
Repacked from 4.7 MB to 1.4 MB by dropping the 82 unused animation clips
(the pack ships 95; the game only needs 13). Geometry, skin weights,
skeleton and the retained clips are otherwise untouched — no decimation or
welding, which would corrupt the skinning weights.

Retained clips:
  Idle, Idle_Combat, Walking_A, Walking_B, Running_A,
  1H_Melee_Attack_Chop, 1H_Melee_Attack_Slice_Horizontal,
  Death_A, Death_B, Hit_A, Hit_B,
  Spawn_Ground_Skeletons, Skeletons_Awaken_Floor

Note on draw cost
-----------------
This model is 9 separate skinned meshes (arms / body / cloak / eyes / head /
jaw / legs), so one instance costs 9 draw calls versus 2 for the zombie glb.
That is why it is capped at 3 alive at once (SKELETON_MAX_ALIVE in config.js)
and only spawns on the snow map.
