# Valley ground textures

Copied from `painted-valley/public/textures/` for the 彩绘山谷 map.

- `Ground048_Color.jpg`, `Ground048_NormalGL.jpg`, `Ground103_Color.jpg`,
  `Ground103_NormalGL.jpg` — ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), Ground048 and
  Ground103 1K JPEG maps, **CC0 1.0**. They paint the earth of the footpaths.

The land, sky, clouds, mountains, village and trees themselves are generated in
code by the vendored rendering stack (see `src/painted/LICENSE`), not loaded as
images. No audio from the upstream demo was copied.
